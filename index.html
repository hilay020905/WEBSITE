Selected Projects
01
Jan 2026 — Apr 2026
Monolithic 3D JSCAN Architecture with RL Optimization
Course CS526L · Supervisor: Jayanarayan Tudu

Designed a 4-tier 3D Joint-Scan (JScan) DfT architecture with clustered MSS/PRAS scan structures, dual MISRs, a JTAG wrapper and MIV-based cross-tier connectivity. Built a PPO-based hierarchical multi-agent RL framework for scan-cell clustering, placement, MIV allocation and cross-tier scheduling. Evaluated on S-ISCAS89 against the 2D JScan baseline: 38.3% lower routing congestion, 69.3% lower wirelength, 87.9% lower peak test power and 20% less area, with zero DRC violations and positive timing slack.

02
Jan 2026 — Apr 2026
Configurable MAC Array Accelerator RTL

Architected a parameterizable INT8/INT16 2D MAC accelerator with configurable array dimensions and accumulation depth for GEMM and edge-AI workloads. Added operand isolation and hierarchical clock gating at PE and array level to cut switching activity. Verified with self-checking directed and constrained-random tests in Verilator — 1,280 checks across 20 randomized 8×8×16 matrix multiplications — and built a baseline-vs-optimized flow to confirm functional equivalence.

Course EE311L

Designed a complete RV32IMZicsr processor family covering single-cycle, 5-stage pipelined, dual-core dual-issue superscalar and vector architectures, with hazard detection, forwarding, branch prediction and CSR support. AMBA APB Memory — SystemVerilog Design Verification

Developed and verified an AMBA APB-compliant memory slave (PSEL, PENABLE, PWRITE, PADDR, PWDATA, PRDATA). Built a class-based verification environment with constrained-random stimulus, generator/driver/monitor architecture and mailbox communication, plus a self-checking scoreboard with a reference memory model that catches read/write mismatches and protocol errors. Debugged with waveforms in ModelSim/QuestaSim.

01

Correctness before cleverness. Every design must be verifiable. A fast chip that misbehaves is just a fast bug. Simulation, formal methods, and testbench discipline are not afterthoughts — they are the work.

02

ML and hardware are converging. Reinforcement learning for physical design, neural branch predictors, AI-assisted EDA — the next decade of chip design will be driven by learning-based optimisation. I build exactly at that intersection.

03

Publish to advance the field. Good research doesn’t end at the waveform. Every architectural idea deserves scrutiny, and sharing accelerates progress for everyone working on the same hard problems.
